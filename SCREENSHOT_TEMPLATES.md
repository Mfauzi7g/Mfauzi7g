## Text Overlay Design:
- **Background**: Semi-transparent dark overlay
- **Headline Font**: Bold, 48px, White
- **Subtitle Font**: Regular, 32px, Light gray
- **Position**: Top or bottom third of screen

## Colors to Use:
- **Primary**: #4F46E5 (Your app blue)
- **Text**: White on dark overlay  
- **Accent**: #10B981 (Green for positive actions)

## Where to Add Text Overlays:
1. **Canva**: Upload screenshot → Add text
2. **Figma**: Free design tool
3. **Photoshop**: If you have it
4. **Preview (Mac)**: Built-in text tools
